About

Hello, I'm Anie

Most of my time goes into systems with more than one customer inside them. When every organisation shares one database, one storage layer and one codebase, the security of each tenant rests on boundaries I have to build and then prove, so isolation, access control and audit trails are where I spend my attention.

The rest goes into data platforms for the Nigerian energy sector, and full stack product work for clients who need something shipped and hosted, not prototyped. I take each system from data model through deployment, payments and operational security.

I graduated in Computer Science from Caleb University with a Second Class Upper, and I mentor final year students through building and defending complete systems.
