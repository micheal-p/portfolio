About

Hello, I'm Anie.

Most of my time goes into systems with more than one customer inside them.

When every organisation shares one database, one storage layer and one codebase, the security of each tenant rests on boundaries I have to build and then prove. So isolation, access control and audit trails are where my attention goes.

The rest goes into data platforms for the Nigerian energy sector, and product work for clients who need something shipped and hosted, not prototyped. I offer system architecture and software consultancy, and I am currently building an AI, government and policy startup as sole engineer. I graduated in Computer Science from Caleb University with a Second Class Upper, and I mentor final year students through building and defending complete systems.
